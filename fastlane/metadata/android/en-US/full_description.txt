A calculator and unit converter for keypad phones like the Qin F21, Dumber Mini or Doov R77 Pro. There are no on-screen buttons: you type the calculation straight on the keypad and press OK for the result. Touch is switched off inside the app, so nothing gets pressed by accident in your pocket.

<b>Keys</b>
• 0–9 – type numbers
• * – add an operator, press it again for the next one (+ − × ÷)
• Hold * – a palette of symbols laid out like the keypad: ^, √, parentheses, %, ±
• # – decimal point
• OK or Call – show the result
• Back – delete the last character; hold it to clear everything
• ↑ / ↓ – go through the history
• ← / → – switch between Calculator, Unit converter, Currency converter and Settings

<b>Features</b>
• The usual order of operations and exact decimals (0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3)
• History like a paper tape, cleared when you close the app
• Unit converter for length, area, volume, mass, temperature, time, speed, pressure and energy, in both directions
• Currency converter with about 30 currencies at the European Central Bank's rates, works offline with the last ones
• Copy the result, or take a converted value over to the calculator
• System, light, dark, dark red and custom themes, a keypad-friendly color picker, blue or any other icon color
• No ads, no tracking; internet only for the ECB's currency rates

Based on Fossify Calculator (GPL-3.0), keypad input inspired by Calc-U-Later.
